## Your First Session — Where to Actually Start (Fall 2026)

The chapter ends by asking you to name one workflow you would redesign. If you want to run the Maya experiment from the chapter on your own documents *today*:

1. **Get the tools running.** Follow the setup sheet at **cognitioneconomy.net/setup-companion** — Claude (claude.ai, desktop app at claude.ai/download), Gemini (gemini.google.com), and a Gmail account. Twenty to thirty minutes.
2. **Reproduce the Maya scene.** Open claude.ai → new conversation → attach a stack of real documents (the paperclip/attach button accepts PDFs and most office formats; the paid plan handles a genuinely large stack). Ask the chapter's actual prompt shape: *"Identify the three most significant [X] across these documents, with direct quotes."*
3. **Time it.** The point of Module 0 is the economics. Note what the same synthesis would have cost you in hours.

The proper, briefed version of this workflow — with a context brief, model choice, and voice input — is Chapter 1's applied exercise. Its click-paths are on the Chapter 1 companion page.
