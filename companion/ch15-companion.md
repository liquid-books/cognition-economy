## The Two-Minute Knowledge-Tax Lab — Current Examples

The printed lab: time your assistant producing a document your industry still bills days for; ask a colleague what your firm bills for it; the gap is the tax, measured today.

Fall 2026 seed examples readers have logged (yours will differ — that is the point):

- Six-page regulatory-inquiry response memo: ~90 seconds to first full draft on a frontier model vs. ~2.5 associate-days billed.
- Three-competitor market brief with sourced citations: under 10 minutes with live search vs. a 1–2 day analyst turnaround.
- First-pass multi-entity depreciation schedule review: minutes vs. hours.
