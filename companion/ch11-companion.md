## Track C — Agent-Orchestration Workspace

- **Setup:** Install the Antigravity IDE; the overview at **antigravity.google/docs/ide-overview** walks through the install. ("Free during preview" no longer applies — check current plan terms.)
- **Step 1 (open the orchestration view):** Press **CMD+E** (Mac) or **CTRL+E** (Windows) to switch from the editor to the **Agent Manager** view. Confirm you are in the orchestration view, not the editor — the doc page above shows both surfaces.
- **Step 5 (review the artifacts):** In Agent Manager, deliverables appear as **markdown Artifacts** attached to the task timeline. Click a task's tile to open its artifacts.
- **Step 6 (export):** Use the artifact's export/share control to save the final brief as a markdown file.
- Steps 2–4 are fully in print — no perishable details.
