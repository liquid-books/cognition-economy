## The Three-Tool Stack — Current Mapping (Fall 2026)

| Book role | Current product | Where to get it | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tool 1 — Thinking partner | **Claude** (Anthropic) + **Claude Desktop** app | claude.ai · desktop app at claude.ai/download | Free tier available; **Claude Pro ≈ \$20/month** (≈ \$17/month billed annually) unlocks the balanced and flagship models and is the plan this course assumes |
| Tool 2 — Ecosystem-native assistant | **Gemini** (Google) | gemini.google.com | Free tier covers the early modules; the paid consumer tier is sold under Google's current AI plan branding (bundled with Google One) at a comparable ≈ \$20/month — plan names have changed within the past year, so check the plan page rather than this table |
| Tool 3 — Sandbox + API key | **Google AI Studio** | aistudio.google.com | Free (API usage beyond the free quota is pay-as-you-go) |

**Why the desktop app is required, not optional:** MCP tool connections and the connectors panel live in Claude Desktop, not the browser tab. Later modules depend on it.

**Already paying for Google Workspace at the business level?** Some Gemini access may be included in your organization's plan. Check before subscribing separately.

## Everything Else on the Printed Checklist

- **A modern browser** — Chrome is the safest choice for this course: the browser-agent exercises in Chapter 3 use Claude in Chrome, and Google's OAuth flows are tested there first.
- **A Gmail address** — free at gmail.com. Required for the Google connectors in Chapter 3 and the Gems in Chapter 2.
- **A text editor** — any plain-text editor. **VS Code** is the default recommendation: free at code.visualstudio.com.

## What Currently Requires a Paid Plan (Fall 2026)

- **Modules 0–4:** completable on free tiers, with patience — free-tier usage caps on Claude are the main friction.
- **Module 2 onward (recommended):** Claude Pro. The Pro plan includes **Cowork**, the agentic workspace used in later chapters, and materially higher usage limits.
- **Module 5 onward:** a paid plan on at least one vendor is effectively necessary for the longer exercises.
- **Chapter 3's browser agent (Claude in Chrome):** paid plans only at press time.
- **Gemini paid tier:** genuinely optional until the long-context work in Module 5.

**Budget guidance unchanged from the book:** one paid consumer AI plan, roughly the price of a streaming subscription per month. If cost is a constraint, flag it — there are always workarounds.
