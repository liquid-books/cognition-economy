## Answer Key

1. **B** — A small wrong assumption at step one (like the wrong target customer) becomes a completely wrong output by the time the AI finishes. The AI never knew it was off course — it succeeded at the wrong task.

2. **C** — Plan Mode is a practice, not a feature. It deliberately separates thinking (planning) from doing (executing) so the AI externalizes its reasoning before producing any output.

3. **B** — During planning, the AI can look but cannot touch. It reads, analyzes, and questions — but all actual drafting, building, and deciding is deferred until the plan is reviewed and approved.

4. **C** — An approved plan is a contract: it locks in scope, sequence, assumptions, and success criteria. If execution later goes wrong, you have a reference point to diagnose whether the problem was in the plan or in carrying it out.

5. **B** — Plan Mode pays off most when a task is complex (many steps and decisions) and when the output is hard to undo (high cost of being wrong). When both conditions are present, planning is the professional standard.

6. **C** — Use your most powerful model to think deeply through the plan, then hand the approved plan to a faster model for execution. You get the architect's judgment at planning time and the builder's throughput at execution time.

7. **C** — The first plan surfaces a few issues. After refinement and reuse, the plan improves with each iteration. Over time, recurring tasks accumulate a battle-tested framework that works reliably — individual productivity compounding into organizational-level intelligence.

8. **C** — Ultraplan is for genuinely high-stakes work. The planning phase becomes its own project: state the objective, get a comprehensive plan, push back on assumptions, sleep on it, review again with fresh eyes. Only when the plan holds up under repeated scrutiny do you commit to execution.

9. **B** — The plan defines the handoffs between specialized agents: what each agent receives, what it produces, and in what order. Without a plan, multi-step multi-agent work is chaos; with one, it becomes a repeatable production system.

10. **C** — Architects blueprint before builders break ground. Surgeons review scans before picking up a scalpel. Pilots file flight plans before pushback. In every high-stakes profession, planning is non-negotiable — and AI's speed and capability make this principle *more* critical, not less.
