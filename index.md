## What This Book Is About

For the past century, competitive advantage meant one thing: **information asymmetry**. If you knew something the market didn't, you could charge for the gap. That moat held for generations.

Then the internet handed every villager a drawbridge controller.

And now AI is rolling a siege engine up to the last walls that remained.

**This is not another "AI tools" book.** It is not a guide to writing better prompts. It is not a list of apps. It is a masterclass in *redesigning work* — your work, your team's work, your organization's work — for the era in which cognition is rentable.

By the time you close this book, you will not just *use* AI. You will *build around it*.

## The Two Ideas That Run Through Every Chapter

::::{grid} 1 1 2 2
:gutter: 3

:::{grid-item-card} 💡 Mental Model #1 — LLM = Pure IQ
An LLM is **intelligence**, not knowledge. Think of Claude Opus 4.7 as a person with an IQ of roughly 180. Einstein clocked in around 160. For \$20/month, you now have the most powerful reasoning partner in human history — but it only knows what you put in front of it.

*Intelligence is what the model brings. Knowledge is what you bring.*
:::

:::{grid-item-card} 🔦 Mental Model #2 — The Flashlight Theory
*(Credit: Matty Squarzoni)*

Imagine that 180-IQ mind locked in a pitch-black room. You ask, *"What's on the desk?"* It cannot answer — not because it isn't smart, but because it cannot **see** anything.

Flash a beam of light and ask again. Now it explains, contextualizes, synthesizes.

**Context is the flashlight beam.** Every technique in this book is a way to aim a better one.
:::

## Who This Book Is For

This book was written for **business people** — period.

- The undergraduate who wants to graduate already operating like a pro
- The MBA student who wants to lead, not be replaced
- The operator who needs to ship, not theorize
- The founder staring at a blank screen and a \$20 subscription
- The manager being asked to "do something with AI"
- The marketing director, the sales lead, the finance analyst, the consultant

You do **not** need a computer science background. You do **not** need to have written code. If you can use a web browser and read this paragraph, you have everything you need.

---

## How to Read This Book

Every chapter follows the same rhythm:

1. **Intuition first** — an analogy, a short story, or a mental model
2. **Detail second** — only after you *get it* do we go deeper
3. **Applied exercise last** — do something real before you turn the page

The book uses two recurring analogies — the **IQ in a box** and the **Flashlight Theory** — that weave through every module. Once you have them, everything else clicks into place.

**Suggested pace:** One chapter per day. Finish each exercise before moving on. The book compounds; skipping the exercises costs you more than you think.

## About the Author

**Dr. Ernesto Lee** is an educator, author, and entrepreneur whose work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and business. He is the author of [*AI and the Art of Productive Struggle*](https://www.amazon.com/AI-Art-Productive-Struggle-Reshapes/dp/B0FJ7MN1ZW/) and [*The Apex Rulebook*](https://www.amazon.com/APEX-Rule-Book-Brutal-Predator/dp/B0GVS3CNBH/), holds faculty appointments at major institutions, and has built and exited several technology ventures.

His teaching focuses on what he calls *productive struggle* — the belief that real learning happens when authentic cognitive challenge meets the right tools.
