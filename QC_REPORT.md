### Quiz 10 — Question 9 issue (FLAGGED)

The question asks: "The chapter describes a 'maturity arc' for how professionals come to use agent teams. Which TWO of the following statements correctly describe this arc?"

Options:
- A) Stage one is using only solo agents ⭐ (marked correct)
- B) Stage two is using teams for everything ⭐ (marked correct)
- C) Stage three is using teams exclusively (wrong — chapter rejects this)
- D) Four stages with teams managed by teams (wrong — fabrication)
- E) Stage three is default-solo with teams for high-stakes parallel problems

**Problem:** Option E is *also* a correct description of stage three per the chapter. The author noticed this and added a "NOTE TO STUDENT" inside the explanation saying both (A, B) and (B, E) are acceptable answers. This is a stop-gap and not a clean exam question. The format requires exactly 2 correct answers and currently has 3 defensible ones.

**Recommended fix (for Dr. Lee):** Either
1. Replace option E with a clearly wrong distractor (e.g., "Stage three is recursive — teams of teams managing teams of teams"), or
2. Convert the question to "Which THREE" and mark A, B, E correct (less clean), or
3. Tighten the wording of option E so it becomes wrong (e.g., add a falsifying clause).

**Did NOT auto-fix** because this requires authorial judgment on how to rewrite the question or distractor.
