# Required Setup — The Five-Minute Prerequisites

Before Chapter 1, you need a working environment. This section tells you *what* to get and *why*. The *where* — current plan names, prices, and download links — lives on the setup sheet at `drlee.io/setup` and in Appendix F, because those details change faster than a book can be reprinted.

Plan for 20–30 minutes to complete this the first time.

---

## The Three-Tool Stack

This course runs on three tools. You will use all three, and you will come to understand *why* you use all three — each one has a distinct role. For now, just get them running.

### Tool 1 — Your Thinking Partner (today: Claude)

**What it is:** Your primary AI workspace — the tool used for most reasoning, writing, and agent tasks throughout this course.

**What you need:** A paid consumer plan from Anthropic (about the price of a streaming subscription) and Anthropic's desktop application. The desktop app matters because later modules connect this tool to outside tools and files, and that connection lives in the desktop client rather than the browser tab. Current plan names, prices, and the download link are on the setup sheet at `drlee.io/setup`.

### Tool 2 — Your Ecosystem-Native Assistant (today: Gemini)

**What it is:** Your long-context and multimodal workhorse. Google's flagship model handles documents, PDFs, and large-scale analysis tasks where context length is the constraint — and it lives inside the ecosystem where much of your email, calendar, and file life already happens.

**What you need:** A Google account (you likely already have one) and access to Google's assistant. The free tier covers the early modules; the setup sheet tells you when a paid plan becomes worth it and what it is currently called.

### Tool 3 — Your Sandbox and API Key (today: Google AI Studio)

**What it is:** The third slot in your stack is a *sandbox* — a place where you can see the raw machinery (system prompts, temperature, an API key) without the consumer polish. Chapter 2 explains why every professional stack needs one and walks you through setting it up.

**For now:** Make sure Tools 1 and 2 are running. That's all you need for Modules 0 and 1.

---

## Other Requirements

**A modern browser** — Chrome or Firefox, updated to current version. Several web-based exercises require it.

**A Gmail address** — Most integrations and exercises assume Google-based email. If you don't have one, create a free Gmail account before Chapter 1.

**A text editor** — Any plain text editor works. VS Code is recommended if you don't have a preference; the setup sheet has the link.

---

## Verify Your Setup

Before starting Chapter 1, confirm:

- [ ] You can log into your thinking partner (Tool 1) in the browser and start a conversation
- [ ] Its desktop application is installed and opens on your machine
- [ ] You can log into your ecosystem-native assistant (Tool 2)
- [ ] You have a Gmail address active
- [ ] You have a text editor installed

If any of these fail, resolve them before moving on. The early exercises assume all five are working.
